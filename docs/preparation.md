# Get ready

## Morning: work in groups

- Join a group of about four.
- Have a flipchart, markers, post-its and **one complete usable set of supplied Flow cards per group**.
- Open this site on a shared screen or your own device. No account, paid software licence or printed worksheet is needed for paper activities.

## Afternoon: work individually

1. Bring a computer with a browser and reliable internet.
2. Sign in to [Power Automate](https://make.powerautomate.com) with your own organisational work account.
3. Confirm the instructor-approved environment and permission to create a cloud flow.
4. Open your own Outlook work mailbox. Use your own Office 365 Outlook connection.
5. Confirm which organisation-approved Generative AI app may be used in the final activity.

**Access must be confirmed before training:** Power Automate cloud-flow entitlement, Exchange Online mailbox access, connector permissions and approved AI access. The email lab uses a Standard connector and requires no Premium connector or paid Copilot add-on; that does not guarantee your account has access.

::: details If access is blocked
Ask the instructor or TA. Do not share an account, buy a subscription or change organisation policies. Follow the demonstration and write a test plan. Mark your result **observed only / not run on my account**. For AI, use the [written fallback review](/resources/fallback).
:::

## Keep practice safe

Send test emails only to yourself. Use fictional examples; do not paste real customer information, private messages, credentials or raw run history into an AI app. Keep your own result notes private.
