# Choose a challenge

Attempt any challenge in any order. Start with **4 or 5** if unsure. Read **Scenario → Challenge → Checkpoint**, then build and explain the normal path under **Required for 1 point**. The complete scenario includes another outcome; that outcome and any technical-limit discussion are optional. Completing all six challenges is optional.

## Keep score together

Use one shared board with group names as rows and **Challenge 1–6** as columns. Only the instructor/TA marks a challenge after accepting your group’s starting Trigger, connected cards and the outcome under **Required for 1 point** in its Checkpoint. Each challenge can earn its point only once. Total the accepted columns: **0–6 points**.

Ask for hints and retry as often as needed without penalty. There are no partial points or speed bonuses; tied highest scores share the win. Optional branches and technical-limit discussions do not affect your score or earn extra points.
