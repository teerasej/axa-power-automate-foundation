## Keep score together

Use one shared board with group names as rows and **Challenge 1–6** as columns. Ask the instructor/TA to mark each accepted challenge once. Total the accepted columns: **0–6 points**.

Hints and retries have no penalty. No speed bonuses; tied highest scores share the result. The score reflects a basic paper simulation only.
