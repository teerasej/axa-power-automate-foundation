# Challenge 2 — Monthly review and approval

![A business owner reviews a monthly report while colleagues await confirmation.](/images/challenge-02-monthly-review-approval.png)

## Scenario

At the start of each month, a team reviews assumptions used in an internal report. A business owner must decide whether to approve the review before the team is told it is approved.

Use fictional information only. These are training examples, not organisational policies. This challenge is a physical-card simulation; no software account or paid licence is required.

## Goal

Start on the schedule, obtain a human approval and notify the team of that approval.

## What you need

Supplied Flow cards, post-its and your group. Use only available cards; notes may explain repeated uses, roles or inputs.

## Normal test case

The review starts on the first day of each month at 09:00. The business owner responds Approve. The team receives confirmation.

## Steps

1. Identify the scheduled start and responsible role.
2. Place the human approval request after the start.
3. Show that the approved notification depends on the approval outcome.
4. Walk the Approve case and request a basic-sequence check.

## Check your result

The schedule starts the process and a human approval precedes the approved notification.

An instructor/TA awards one point for an accepted basic sequence, only once for this challenge. Detailed configuration and a complete exception route are not required. Hints and retries are allowed without deductions.

::: details Optional exception discussion

If the owner chooses Reject, how should the coordinator hear about it? Who follows up if the owner does not respond?


:::
