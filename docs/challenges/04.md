# Challenge 4 — Internal resource approval

![A coordinator presents an equipment request to a budget owner while operations awaits the approved handover.](/images/challenge-04-internal-resource-approval.png)

## Scenario

A coordinator receives an internal resource request that has already passed an initial information check. The coordinator must start the workflow manually, send the request to the budget owner for a decision, and notify the relevant team of the outcome.

The request details, amount, and approver used in this activity are fictional and do not represent an actual policy.

<span id="your-challenge"></span>
<span id="arrange-and-explain"></span>

## Challenge

Arrange the Flow cards so that the coordinator starts the workflow, requests approval, and separates the **Approved** and **Rejected** outcomes. Each path must have a clearly identified recipient.

> **Boundary:** Use only the node cards in the physical deck. Record policy checks, approver selection, and sign-off as human responsibilities on sticky notes, not as new Actions.

> **For this game:** The complete workflow includes both outcomes. Build and explain the normal path under **Required for 1 point** first. The other outcome is optional and earns no extra points.

<span id="try-the-normal-case"></span>
<span id="check-your-result"></span>

## Checkpoint

**Required for 1 point:** Show the coordinator starting the workflow manually, the budget owner making the approval decision and the relevant team receiving the approved outcome. Identify the recipient and explain that the system communicates the decision rather than making it.

Explain your valid starting Trigger, connected supplied cards and normal result to the instructor/TA. Only the instructor/TA records the accepted point.

**Optional discussion:** Walk through a `Reject` decision, identify the recipient of the rejection notice and show that the request is not passed on for action. The budget owner remains responsible for the decision.

You can earn the point without completing the optional discussion or recalling a technical limitation.
