# Challenge 4 — Internal resource approval

![A coordinator presents an equipment request to a budget owner before an operations handover.](/images/challenge-04-internal-resource-approval.png)

## Scenario

A coordinator has already checked the information in an internal resource request. They start the process manually and ask the budget owner to decide before notifying operations.

Use fictional information only. These are training examples, not organisational policies. This challenge is a physical-card simulation; no software account or paid licence is required.

## Goal

Start manually, obtain a human approval and tell operations to begin the next step.

## What you need

Supplied Flow cards, post-its and your group. Use only available cards; notes may explain repeated uses, roles or inputs.

## Normal test case

Fictional request REQ-104 asks for equipment for an internal activity. The budget owner responds Approve. Operations receives the approved instruction.

## Steps

1. Identify what the coordinator checks before starting.
2. Place a valid manual start before the approval request.
3. Show that operations is notified only after the human approval outcome.
4. Walk REQ-104 through the cards and request a basic-sequence check.

## Check your result

A person starts the process and a different human responsibility supplies the approval; the system does not make the budget decision.

An instructor/TA awards one point for an accepted basic sequence, only once for this challenge. Detailed configuration and a complete exception route are not required. Hints and retries are allowed without deductions.
