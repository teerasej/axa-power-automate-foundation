# Challenge 3 — Field-support request routing

![A field colleague submits a support request while an office team receives its details.](/images/challenge-03-field-support-routing.png)

## Scenario

The field team submits support requests through a form. Each request must include a subject, contact details, urgency level, and description. The central team must read the response and separate requests that are ready to proceed from requests with missing information.

Use fictional requesters and contact details only.

<span id="your-challenge"></span>
<span id="arrange-and-explain"></span>

## Challenge

Arrange the Flow cards to receive a form response, retrieve its details, and direct the request to one of two outcomes:

- Complete information: notify the receiving team in Microsoft Teams.
- Missing information: send an email asking for the missing details.

> **Boundary:** Use only the node cards in the physical deck. Do not add an Action for saving to a database, assigning work, or updating a status because those cards are not available.

> **For this game:** The complete workflow includes both outcomes. Build and explain the normal path under **Required for 1 point** first. The other outcome is optional and earns no extra points.

<span id="try-the-normal-case"></span>
<span id="check-your-result"></span>

## Checkpoint

**Required for 1 point:** Receive a complete form response, retrieve its details and use those details to notify the receiving team in Microsoft Teams. The message includes the subject, contact details, urgency level and description; use the answers only after retrieving the response details.

Explain your valid starting Trigger, connected supplied cards and normal result to the instructor/TA. Only the instructor/TA records the accepted point.

**Optional discussion:** Walk through a request with missing information and show how the requester receives an email asking for those details.

You can earn the point without completing the optional discussion or recalling a technical limitation.
